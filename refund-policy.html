1. Introduction

At StayStillScaleHigh, we are committed to delivering high-quality content, resources, and community experiences to every member. We understand that circumstances can change, and this policy is designed to be fair and transparent about how we handle refund requests.

Due to the nature of our Services — which include immediate access to digital content, live sessions, community spaces, and proprietary resources — our refund policy is structured to reflect the value delivered from the moment membership begins.

2. General Refund Policy

All membership fees paid to StayStillScaleHigh are generally non-refundable once a billing cycle has commenced and access to the Platform has been granted. This applies to all membership tiers — monthly, quarterly, and annual.

By completing your purchase, you acknowledge that you are gaining immediate access to digital content and community resources, and that this forms the basis of our non-refundable policy.

We strongly encourage all prospective members to review the Platform's features, membership benefits, and available information before subscribing. If you have any questions about what is included, please enquire before completing your purchase.

4. Membership Cancellation

4.1 How to Cancel

You may cancel your membership at any time by logging into your account and navigating to the membership settings section. Cancellation requests must be submitted through the Platform to be processed.

4.2 Effect of Cancellation

  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.
  • You retain full access to the Platform and all membership benefits until the end of the paid period.
  • No partial refunds are issued for the unused days remaining in the billing cycle at the time of cancellation.
  • After cancellation, your account will revert to a non-member status and access to premium content and community spaces will be removed.

4.3 Auto-Renewal

Memberships renew automatically at the end of each billing cycle. To avoid being charged for a renewal, you must cancel your membership at least 24 hours before your next renewal date. Renewal charges that have already been processed are subject to this Refund Policy and are generally non-refundable.

5. Exceptional Circumstances

We recognise that genuinely exceptional circumstances may arise. Refund requests outside our standard policy may be considered at our sole discretion in the following situations:

  • A verifiable and serious medical emergency that prevents you from accessing the Platform
  • A documented technical failure on our part that substantially prevented access for an extended period
  • An accidental duplicate charge or billing error on our part

Exceptional refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of the triggering event and must include supporting documentation where applicable. Approval of such requests is at the sole discretion of StayStillScaleHigh and does not create a precedent for future requests.

We do not consider the following as grounds for exceptional refunds: dissatisfaction with content quality, inability to attend live sessions, failure to use the Platform, or a change in personal or business circumstances.

6. Event and Workshop Purchases

6.1 Event Tickets Purchased Separately

Where tickets to events, workshops, or masterclasses are purchased separately from a membership:

  • Cancellations made more than 7 days before the event date are eligible for a full refund of the ticket price.
  • Cancellations made within 7 days of the event are not eligible for a refund but may be transferred to another person with prior written approval.
  • No-shows are not eligible for a refund.

6.2 Events Cancelled by StayStillScaleHigh

In the event that StayStillScaleHigh cancels a scheduled event, all ticket holders will receive either a full refund or a credit of equal value toward a future event, at the purchaser's choice.

6.3 Event Rescheduling

If an event is rescheduled, ticket holders will be notified and offered the option to retain their ticket for the new date or receive a full credit toward a future event. Refunds for rescheduled events are not automatically issued but may be considered on request.

7. How to Submit a Refund Request

To submit a refund request, please follow these steps:

  1. Log in to your StayStillScaleHigh member account.
  2. Navigate to the Help or Support section of your account portal.
  3. Submit a refund request with the following information:
    • Your full name and registered account details
    • The date of purchase and the amount charged
    • The reason for your refund request
    • Any supporting documentation (if applicable)

We will acknowledge your refund request within 48 hours and aim to provide a resolution within 7 business days. Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 7–10 business days, subject to your bank or payment provider's processing times.

8. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

We encourage members to contact us directly before initiating a chargeback or payment dispute with their bank or card issuer. Most issues can be resolved quickly and amicably through direct communication.

Initiating a chargeback without first contacting us may result in the immediate suspension of your account pending investigation. If a chargeback is found to be unjustified, we reserve the right to recover the disputed amount through other means and to permanently terminate the associated membership without refund.

We cooperate fully with payment processors and financial institutions in the investigation of disputes and will provide all relevant transaction records as required.

9. Currency and Processing

All membership fees are charged in the currency displayed at the time of purchase. Refunds, where approved, will be issued in the same currency as the original transaction. StayStillScaleHigh is not responsible for currency conversion fees, exchange rate fluctuations, or bank processing fees that may apply to your refund.

Processing times for approved refunds may vary depending on your payment provider and are typically between 7–10 business days from the date of approval.

10. Changes to This Refund Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We will notify active members of material changes through the Platform or by other reasonable means.

Continued use of the Platform following the posting of any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised Refund Policy. The version of this policy in effect at the time of your most recent purchase governs any refund requests arising from that purchase.

11. Contact Information

For questions about this Refund Policy or to submit a refund request, please use the support form available in your member account portal or on our official website.

We will endeavour to respond to all enquiries within 2–3 business days.
