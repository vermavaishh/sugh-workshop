Where this goes next

The hard part is already working. Getting a small page in front of thousands of new people is the difficult half, and it is happening — 4,800 views a month with no ad spend. The next phase is about turning more of that attention into followers and orders.

  • A consistent grid. One template, one type style and one colour family across designed posts so the page reads as a proper brand the moment someone lands on it.
  • Plain-background product photos. Shooting against a plain wall instead of among the racks will lift every post — and costs nothing.
  • A reason to follow, in the bio. A line like "new arrivals every week" gives visitors a reason to stay, not just a description of what is sold.
  • Weekly content batch. Three or four videos and a set of photos every Monday keeps the rhythm unbroken — the one thing that reliably drives the numbers up.
  • Track WhatsApp enquiries. Noting roughly how many orders come in each week turns the next report into a direct sales story.
